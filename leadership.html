Where I Stepped Up

01

School Governance

Student Council Member

Duration: 2 Years

A multi-year commitment representing student interests, coordinating across classes, and executing school-wide initiatives.

Serving on the Student Council for two consecutive years was an essential training ground in practical collaboration. Rather than just holding a title, the role demanded day-to-day dependability, clear communication, and the patience to coordinate with students and faculty.

Core Responsibilities & Contributions

  • Event Planning & Execution: Helped plan and organise school events and student competitions from early coordination through final wrap-up.
  • Peer Collaboration: Worked alongside other council members to distribute workloads, resolve logistical bottlenecks, and ensure smooth operations.
  • Operational Accountability: Managed assigned responsibilities consistently and contributed actively to broader student body activities and campus culture.

As Vice Captain of the school house, leadership was about active motivation and cross-age coordination. I worked directly on the ground to rally students, bridge communications between staff and peers, and cultivate genuine team spirit across competitions.

Core Responsibilities & Contributions

  • House Coordination: Coordinated students and inter-house activities, scheduling rehearsals and preparing participants for sports and cultural contests.
  • Fostering Participation: Encouraged student participation across all grades, motivating peers who were hesitant to step forward.
  • Faculty & Peer Liaison: Acted as a dependable link between house teachers, the house captain, and students to maintain discipline and shared focus.
  • Team Performance: Contributed to a house that worked cohesively and won various competitive events throughout the academic year.
