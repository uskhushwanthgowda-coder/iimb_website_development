Personal Philosophy

I'm Khushwanth — currently pursuing a BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore.

I'm someone who enjoys exploring ideas, understanding how things work and figuring out why some things succeed while others don't. My interests lie particularly in business, entrepreneurship and the possibilities that emerge when a good idea meets the right execution.

Over the years, I've had opportunities to take responsibility, work with different people, organise events and experience leadership beyond the classroom. These experiences have shaped how I approach teamwork, responsibility and new challenges.

Turning Point

A Change of Direction

An important turning point came when I decided to step away from engineering college and pursue a direction that felt more closely aligned with my interests in business and entrepreneurship. It taught me to make independent choices and take responsibility for the path I wanted to explore.

I'm still discovering what I'm capable of, what I want to build and where my interests will take me. For now, I'm focused on learning, experimenting and making the most of the opportunities in front of me.
